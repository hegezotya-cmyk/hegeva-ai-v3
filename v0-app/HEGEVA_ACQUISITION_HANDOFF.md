# HEGEVA acquisition landing pages — V1 handoff

Positioning: **The AI Operating System for Your Business.** The five pages support UK search discovery without repositioning HEGEVA as a chatbot, invoice-only product or trade-specific compliance system.

| Route | Primary UK search intent | Conversion focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/ai-for-small-business` | AI tools for small-business operations | Customers, quotes, invoices, priorities and Opportunity Radar |
| `/ai-business-assistant` | Business-aware AI assistant | Structured workspace context and controlled next actions |
| `/quote-and-invoice-software` | Quote and invoice workflow software | Quote → follow-up → invoice → payment attention |
| `/ai-for-trades` | AI/admin help for UK trades | Recover existing leads and reduce missed follow-ups |
| `/ai-for-electricians` | Business admin AI for electricians | Enquiry-to-payment workflow with explicit compliance boundary |

All pages use unique titles, descriptions, H1s, differentiated copy, internal links, canonical URLs, Open Graph metadata, FAQ content, BreadcrumbList and WebApplication structured data. Public examples contain no fabricated customer results or testimonials. Authenticated workspace data is never used on these public pages.

The CTA path is real: landing page → HEGEVA registration/login → Command Center. Pricing links use the verified Premium £14.99/month and Pro £29.99/month values. Local, capped, first-party attribution records only landing-page view, registration-start and pricing-view event names, route and timestamp; it records no email, customer, invoice, document or message content and sends nothing to an advertising platform.

Owner follow-up after deployment:

1. Verify `https://hegevaai.co.uk` in Google Search Console using the owner's Google account or DNS method.
2. Submit `https://hegevaai.co.uk/sitemap.xml`.
3. Review indexing and search-query data before creating any Google Ads campaign.
4. Select a consent-aware analytics service and account if cross-device aggregate funnel measurement is required. Do not add an invented analytics ID.

No Google Ads campaign was created, no budget was accepted and no advertising spend was initiated in this package.
