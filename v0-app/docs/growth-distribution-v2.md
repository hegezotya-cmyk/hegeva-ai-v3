## Publishing rules

1. Organic only. No paid promotion.
2. Use only the three approved FINAL HEGEVA videos.
3. Do not change an already-published V1 link merely to make analytics cleaner.
4. New posts use the V2 links above.
5. Trade-group posts use the matching niche page, not the homepage.
6. Helpful/problem-solving posts use /free-tools.
7. Broad HEGEVA/product posts use /challenge.
8. Do not claim guaranteed savings, guaranteed revenue, or guaranteed customer growth.
9. Do not present fictional challenge customer names, invoice amounts or the demo score as the visitor's own data.
10. After each posting batch, inspect GA4 before changing product copy.
