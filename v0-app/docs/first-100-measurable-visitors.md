# HEGEVA — First 100 Measurable Visitors Plan

Date: 2026-09-19

Goal: generate the first 100 measurable organic visits to the new Growth V1 entry points without paid advertising.

This is a traffic target, not a guarantee. Quality and conversion matter more than raw clicks.

## Daily guardrails

- Organic only.
- Respect every group/subreddit/platform rule.
- Do not post identical copy repeatedly in the same community.
- Do not mass-DM strangers.
- Do not ask for fake reviews or fake upvotes.
- Do not claim guaranteed results.
- Reply to genuine questions and criticism.
- Record what was posted and the matching V2 link.
- Review GA4 before changing product copy.

## Day 7 — Evidence review

Do not add a new feature before reading the funnel.

Classify the bottleneck:

A. Low sessions:
Distribution problem.
Action: improve channel targeting, group selection and posting frequency.

B. Sessions but low challenge_start:
Landing/hero/CTA problem.
Action: test clearer promise and stronger first screen.

C. challenge_start but low challenge_complete:
Challenge flow problem.
Action: reduce friction or clarify steps.

D. challenge_complete but low try_my_business_click:
Value/trust/signup handoff problem.
Action: improve proof, trust and CTA.

E. registration_start but low completion:
Registration/verification problem.
Action: inspect signup friction and email delivery.

F. registrations but low own_business_result:
Onboarding/First WOW problem.
Action: improve first real-input experience.

## 100-visitor checkpoint

At 100 measurable organic sessions, record:

- sessions
- users
- engaged sessions
- source / medium
- campaign content
- challenge_start
- challenge_complete
- try_my_business_click
- registration_start
- registration_completed
- own_business_start
- own_business_result
- free_tool_use
- share_click
- referral_visit
- referral_signup

Do not evaluate a channel from one click. Use enough traffic to distinguish a real pattern from noise.

## Success definition

The first 100-visitor experiment succeeds if it gives enough clean data to identify where the acquisition funnel is strongest and where it loses users.

A paying customer is the commercial objective, but this 100-visitor checkpoint is primarily an evidence-gathering milestone.
